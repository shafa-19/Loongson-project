==============================================
The bomb blinks its code on the 8 LEDs, one wire
at a time.  Repeat the numbers in the same order
and the next fuse is cut.  You have 90 seconds,
three wrong wires and it goes off.

Time: 90 s, fuses: 5, strikes allowed: 3
