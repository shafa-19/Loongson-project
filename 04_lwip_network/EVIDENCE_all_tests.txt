Build notes (differences that matter):
 * the project must be compiled with -DUSE_LWIP -DBSP_USE_GMAC0, otherwise
   the lwIP/GMAC initialisation code is compiled out and the board stays
   silent on the network (this cost one debugging round).
 * console_init() must stay at 115200 at boot: at 9600 the boot-time
   rt_kprintf in Clock_initialize takes ~36 ms with interrupts disabled and
   the pending ticks then raise "TLB Load" - the console is switched to
   9600 later, inside main(), for the CH340 adapter.
